What this agent will not do

It will not answer from outside the lesson you selected — ask something the lesson does not cover and it refuses, then gives one hint. That is the design, not a failure. Grounding is checked lexically against the lesson text, so a question phrased with none of the lesson's vocabulary is treated as outside it; it errs toward refusing.

Every question and every refusal is logged, so the refusal rate is measurable.
